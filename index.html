Quick answers.

Keep your setup consistent, test changes patiently, and never expect one preset to fit every device.

Is this an official Free Fire website?

No. AimVault Sensi is an independent fan-made sensitivity resource and is not affiliated with or endorsed by Garena.

Will these values guarantee headshots?

No sensitivity can guarantee results. Use the preset as a starting point and combine it with practice, stable FPS, and good crosshair placement.

How often should I change my sensi?

Avoid changing everything daily. Test one value at a time for several matches before deciding whether it helps.

What should I do if my device lags?

Prioritize stable performance: close background apps, use a comfortable graphics setting, and choose consistency over maximum visual quality.
